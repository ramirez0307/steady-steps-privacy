Privacy Policy for Steady Steps

Effective date: October 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Steady Steps (package name com.zaelquorivian.dailyhabitcounter) accesses, uses, stores, and shares information. Questions about this policy may be sent to zaelquorivian@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Steady Steps stores the habits you create, goals, schedules, reminders, completion history, streaks, achievements, theme preferences, and related app settings locally on your device. Steady Steps does not require an account and does not upload this habit information to a server operated by the developer.

Android may transfer eligible local app data directly to a replacement device during device-to-device migration. Steady Steps excludes its local app data from Android cloud backup. If you choose to export a JSON backup or CSV report, Android's system file picker saves it only to the location you select. You control and are responsible for exported files.

Notifications and device features

If you enable habit reminders, Steady Steps requests notification permission and uses your reminder schedule to create local notifications. It also receives a device restart event so enabled reminders can be scheduled again. These features do not send your habit information to the developer.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on your region and consent choices, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostic data, and consent choices. They use this information to provide, personalize where permitted, measure, prevent fraud in, and improve advertising services. Steady Steps provides an ad-privacy-choices option when required.

Google's handling of this information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and its information about data from apps that use Google services.

Google Play purchase

Steady Steps offers an optional one-time Google Play purchase to remove advertising. Google Play processes the transaction and payment information. The app receives purchase status and a purchase token so it can grant and restore the ad-free entitlement. The developer does not receive your complete payment-card details. Google handles purchase information according to the Google Privacy Policy.

Sharing and sale of information

The developer does not sell your personal information. Local habit information is not shared with the developer or third parties. Information processed through advertising or purchases is shared with Google and, for advertising, Google's advertising partners as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law.

Data security

Habit information remains within Android's app-specific storage, which is protected by Android's application sandbox. Data handled by Google services is transmitted using the security measures provided by their SDKs and services. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Retention and deletion

Local habit information remains on your device until you delete the applicable habits, clear Steady Steps' app storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files remain wherever you saved them until you delete them. Google retains and deletes advertising and purchase information under its own policies. Because Steady Steps has no user accounts and the developer does not receive your habit database, there is no separate server-side account or habit record for the developer to delete.

For step-by-step instructions, see the data-deletion request page.

Children's privacy

Steady Steps is not directed specifically to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact the email address below.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Steady Steps' features or data practices change. The new policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact zaelquorivian@gmail.com.

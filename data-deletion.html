Steady Steps Data Deletion

Steady Steps does not use user accounts or store your habit data on developer-operated servers. Your habit information is kept locally on your device.

Delete your habit data from your device

  1. Open Steady Steps.
  2. Delete individual habits from the app, or open Android Settings.
  3. In Android Settings, open Apps, select Steady Steps, then select Storage and clear app data. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data.

Clearing app data or uninstalling removes locally stored habits, goals, schedules, reminders, completion history, streaks, achievements, and app settings from that device. It does not delete any JSON backup or CSV report you previously saved; delete those files from the location where you exported them.

Request assistance or deletion

If you need help deleting local data or want to submit a privacy request, email zaelquorivian@gmail.com with the subject “Steady Steps data deletion request.” Please do not send your habit records, backup files, payment details, passwords, or other sensitive information by email.

Data handled by Google services

The free version uses Google AdMob, and an optional ad-removal purchase is processed by Google Play. Google may retain advertising, consent, device-identifier, diagnostic, or purchase information under its own policies. Steady Steps does not receive payment-card details and does not maintain a server-side habit profile. To manage or request deletion of data held by Google, use the privacy and account controls available through your Google Account and Google Play.

Retention

The developer has no separate server-side user account or habit database to retain. Local data remains only until you delete it, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files remain until you delete them. Google retains information it handles according to its own applicable policies.
